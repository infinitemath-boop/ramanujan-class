Privacy Policy

Ramanujan Class, Namakkal, Tamil Nadu

Effective 11 October 2026

Who we are

Ramanujan Class is a mathematics coaching class in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, preparing students for CSIR NET, GATE, IIT JAM, TN SET and PG TRB. This policy covers the Ramanujan Class app and website, which give enrolled students access to lectures, study material, tests and announcements.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your name and batchTo show your account and the material for your course
Your email addressIt is your sign-in ID
Your phone numberTo contact you about the class, and as your first password, which you should change after signing in
Account status and access datesTo decide whether your account is active

Teachers enter these details when they enroll you. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or any other information from your device, and the app shows no advertising.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which keeps them encrypted in transit and at rest. The app also saves a few preferences, such as your last opened page, in your own browser or phone.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own account details.
  • Ramanujan Class teachers can see the details of all enrolled students.
  • Email addresses and phone numbers are never shown to other students.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we share it with no one except Google, which runs Firebase for us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you are enrolled. When you leave, or when you ask us, we delete your account and its details.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time. Write to infinitemath@ramanujanclass.org or call the Course Mentor on 97896 86706.

Children

Our courses are for college students and graduates. A student under 18 is enrolled only with a parent's or guardian's consent.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell enrolled students in the app's announcements.
